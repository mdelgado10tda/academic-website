01 / About

Mathematics across structures and data.

I am affiliated with the Instituto de Matemáticas de la Universidad de Sevilla (IMUS) at the Universidad de Sevilla and the Depto. Matemática Aplicada I, Sevilla, Spain.

My work brings together algebraic and computational perspectives on topology, with a particular interest in the mathematical structures behind persistence.

02 / Research

Algebraic persistence, computational topology.

My research focuses on the development and study of mathematical structures arising in Topological Data Analysis, particularly the algebraic aspects of persistence theory and multiparameter persistence.

I am interested in connections between multipersistence, representation theory, and sheaf theory. My recent work also touches computational topology, especially image analysis and topological properties of nD-pictures.

arXiv preprint · 16 October 2026

arXiv:2610.23838 · Mathematics (math.AT)

A stable multipersistence invariant for ladder modules

Marco Delgado-Garrido, Lars Moberg Salbu, Álvaro Torras-Casas, and Rocío González-Díaz

Abstract PDF

We define an additive functor S that assigns a three-parameter persistence module to every morphism between one-parameter persistence modules. For bar-to-bar morphisms, its image is rectangle-decomposable. We prove that S is stable with respect to the interleaving distance and study its relation to rectangle decompositions.
